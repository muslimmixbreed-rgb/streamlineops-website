Services

Services built around how your business really works

Every engagement starts with how your business runs today. These are the areas where StreamlineOps most often helps.

01

Process review & mapping

Follow a piece of work from first request to finished job, and see every step, hand-off and wait.

For example: Quoting, order handling, onboarding
02

Excel trackers & dashboards

Trackers, logs and one-page summaries with drop-downs, sound formulas and clear warnings.

For example: Job tracker, stock log, weekly summary
03

SOPs & checklists

One-page procedures in plain English, tested on someone new and kept where the work happens.

For example: Month-end, refunds, new starters
04

KPIs & performance

A small set of measures that show whether things are on track, and a short routine to review them.

For example: Jobs overdue, response time, first-time fix
05

Change projects

A PRINCE2-informed approach, scaled for small businesses: clear stages, owners and checkpoints.

For example: New system, team restructure
06

Team routines

Handovers, short huddles and weekly check-ins that keep everyone aligned without filling the diary.

For example: Shift handover, 10-minute stand-up

Our approach

How an engagement works

Straightforward, practical and sized for a small business. You keep everything that's built.

  1. Discover

    An informal conversation about what's frustrating you and what "better" would look like.

  2. Diagnose

    I see the process as it really runs and talk with the people who do the work.

  3. Design

    A short, prioritised plan, with the quick wins first and bigger changes where they pay off.

  4. Embed

    Tools, procedures and measures handed over so your team owns them.
