About

Structure that large organisations rely on, sized for small business

StreamlineOps was set up to bring practical operational discipline to businesses that don't have an operations department.

Experience

StreamlineOps is built on more than ten years of hands-on operations experience in very different settings.

As a head bartender in a supervisory role, I ran busy shifts where a good routine is the difference between a smooth night and a chaotic one. I've also worked inside large, complex operations at British Gas (part of Centrica) and BT, where a single process can pass through several teams and systems before it's finished.

What connects those roles is the part I've always been good at: seeing where work gets stuck, and creating the structure and processes that let people do their jobs well.

Qualifications

I'm a university graduate and PRINCE2 qualified, and I'm currently studying business management, covering marketing, digital marketing and sales management. That gives a rounded view of how operations connect to the rest of a business.
